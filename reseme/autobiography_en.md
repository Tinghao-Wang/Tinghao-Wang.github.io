# Autobiography

Pursuing the Art of Code, Weaving the Digital Future

My name is Tinghao Wang, and I am from Taipei City. I graduated from the Department of Agricultural Chemistry at National Taiwan University. This educational experience cultivated my interest in scientific research and rigorous attitude, and also laid the foundation for logical thinking and experimental spirit in my future work in data analysis and software development.

After graduating from university, I founded the chocolate brand "Funky Chocolate." During the nearly ten years of entrepreneurship, I handled all aspects of the company's operations, which developed my excellent product development, project management, and cross-functional collaboration skills. In the process of running the business, I learned how to effectively learn new things and built my own website, initially learning HTML and CSS syntax, which marked the beginning of my journey into software development. I successfully applied for multiple government programs (SBIR, SBTR, etc.) and secured NT$ 5.4 million in funding. These experiences taught me how to solve various challenges and gave me the ability to independently complete entire projects.

In early 2023, I fully committed myself to learning software engineering. I self-taught Python and completed Harvard's CS50 course and Coursera's Algorithms and Data Structures course, rapidly improving my programming skills. I am proficient in programming languages such as Python, C#, JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3, and have obtained multiple professional certifications. I successfully developed several projects, including a travel website integrated with AI technology (Papago), a blockchain-based crowdfunding platform, a website that quickly converts audio files to text and extracts key points (Professor-X), an application that uses machine learning to identify plant diseases (PlantGuard), and a website for UberEats business partner applications. These projects enabled me to master frontend and backend development as well as blockchain technology, and I gained valuable experience through practical applications.

In 2024, I joined Multi-Dimensional Space Information Ltd. as a Software Engineer. During my tenure, I introduced GitHub Flow and CI/CD automated integration and deployment systems for all company projects, significantly improving team collaboration efficiency, increasing product iteration speed by 15%-20%, and reducing deployment errors by approximately 30%. I also assisted in maintaining the "Government Soil and Water Conservation Application Process Digitalization" system and integrated it with the government single sign-on ISSO API. Additionally, I conducted OWASP security analysis for the company, reviewed and fixed common security vulnerabilities such as XSS, SQL injection, and CSRF through vulnerability scanning, enhancing the system's security level and effectively reducing the risk of cyber attacks. Although it was only four months, my efforts brought significant benefits to the company, and this experience helped me clarify my interests and decide to continue advancing in the fields of AI and Web3.

In September 2024, I joined Damen Technology Consulting Ltd. as a Web3/AI Full-Stack Engineer. Here, I combined my accumulated technical capabilities with product thinking to plan and build the AlphaGroupAgent AI automated research assistant, integrating multi-source data, vector retrieval, and prompt engineering to achieve automated research report compilation and real-time updates. I led the AlphaDAO quantitative trading team to develop the Flashnet high-performance grid trading system, integrated Gate.io API with security risk control mechanisms, increasing capital utilization by more than tenfold. Additionally, I built the DamenKYT Web3 anti-fraud platform, integrating wallet risk assessment, fund flow tracking, automated reporting, and case filing processes to help track stolen funds and accelerate the reporting process. During this period, I drove the MVP implementation for data processing, backend modules, AI integration, and frontend experiences, integrating services including Supabase, OpenAI, Dify, and Gate.io. From architecture design and database integration to user experience and cloud deployment, I fully mastered the complete full-stack development capabilities. This experience allowed me to deeply engage with Web3, AI, and data engineering, and validated my ability to learn quickly and solve problems in real-world projects.

In the future, I hope to continue deepening my expertise in software engineering, particularly in emerging technologies such as AI, Web3, and blockchain. I believe these technologies will lead the future of software development, and I hope to leverage my full-stack development capabilities, product thinking, and project management experience to contribute to these fields and create more meaningful digital products.
